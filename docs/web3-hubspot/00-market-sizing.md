# 00 · Market Sizing — Web3 companies on HubSpot

## Question

How many Web3 companies use HubSpot as their CRM — i.e. how big is the market for selling an
add-on to Web3-industry HubSpot users?

## Bottom line

- **~7,392** Web3/crypto companies use HubSpot (Apollo technographics — a loose upper bound).
- HubSpot is the **dominant** CRM in the segment: **2.4×** Salesforce.
- Filtered to a realistic buyer profile and discounted for keyword-tag noise: **~2,000** is
  the defensible ICP to plan around.

## Apollo data (live technographics, 3 credits)

Query: companies tagged `blockchain / cryptocurrency / web3 / crypto / defi`, crossed with
detected CRM technology.

| Segment | Companies |
|---|---|
| Web3/crypto companies using **HubSpot** | **7,392** |
| Web3/crypto companies using **Salesforce** | 3,105 |
| Total Web3/crypto universe (Apollo keyword tags) | 156,411 |
| HubSpot users, filtered to 11–1,000 employees, staffing excluded | 3,450 |

## Reliability caveats (important)

- The **156,411** universe is inflated — Apollo's keyword tags sweep in staffing agencies,
  consultancies and any company that merely *mentions* crypto (the first results returned were
  literally recruiting firms). Independent trackers put genuinely Web3-native companies at
  ~15,000–25,000 (Crunchbase ~10k blockchain + ~10k crypto; StartupBlink ~3,461 startups).
- Therefore **7,392 is a loose upper bound**, not a count of native product companies. A
  ~35% single-CRM adoption rate against a ~20k native universe is implausibly high.
- **Working estimate:** ~2,000 native Web3 companies on HubSpot (≈10–13% adoption of a
  ~15–25k universe — a realistic rate for a leading CRM). Range 2,000–3,500.
- The ~2,000 rests on an *estimated* ~60% native-precision rate applied to the 3,450 filtered
  set; a 1-credit Apollo sample of ~100 records would replace the estimate with a measured one.

## What it means

- TAM in accounts is **small but concentrated and reachable** (~1–3k), not tens of thousands —
  ideal for an ABM/marketplace motion.
- HubSpot leads its rival 2.4×, so **build HubSpot-first**.
- Buyer profile: marketing/RevOps-led, startup budget, already buying marketplace add-ons.
