# 01 · Market Needs — the gaps HubSpot leaves for Web3

HubSpot is a TradFi B2B CRM. Web3 go-to-market runs on rails it can't see. These are the gaps
the add-on line closes.

## The five gaps

| Gap | What HubSpot can't do | Consequence for the buyer |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | A wallet is "a random string"; one founder appears as 3 Telegram handles, an ENS, an X handle, a GitHub | Can't build one profile of a real prospect |
| **Signal / timing** | No idea a protocol just raised, forked, shipped a contract, lost TVL, or got exploited | Reaches out cold and late, with no trigger |
| **Technographic** | Can't tell who runs which chain, oracle, bridge or tooling | Can't target by tech stack for lead-gen |
| **Channel** | Can't send or track Telegram, X DMs, or LinkedIn — only email | Real conversations live outside the CRM |
| **Attribution** | Cookie-based, breaks on wallets/cross-device; can't tie a campaign to an on-chain action | Can't prove what worked |
