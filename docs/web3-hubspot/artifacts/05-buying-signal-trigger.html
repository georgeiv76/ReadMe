01

The idea: one engine, not four add-ons

A lead is rarely one signal. It's the convergence of fit (does this account match us), timing (is something happening now), and risk (is there a reason to talk today). So the separate discovery add-ons collapse into a single orchestrator.

Consolidation

Stack Finder + Raise Radar + Ship Signal + Risk Trigger → Buying Signal Trigger. Instead of four tools each watching one thing, one engine ingests every signal source, scores the convergence, and emits a single ranked lead with a "why now." It sits upstream of the Multichannel Campaign — discovery feeds activation.

Priority score

priority =
ICP fit  ×  signal strength  ×  recency

ICP fit — does the account match (chain, size, stage, tech)?
Signal strength — how strongly does the event predict a buy? (a raise > a commit)
Recency — fresh events outrank stale ones.

Convergence multiplier: two+ signals on one account in a window jump it to the top. A raise + hiring-security + unaudited-deploy is an A-lead every time.

06

How it feeds activation

Buying Signal Trigger and Multichannel Campaign are two halves of one motion — discovery and activation — both HubSpot-native, both agent-driven, both enrichment-powered.

Buying Signal Trigger
Finds & ranks the lead. Emits it with a "why now" into HubSpot.
→
HubSpot
System of record. Holds the lead, the signal, the score.
→
Multichannel Campaign
Warms & reaches across email · Telegram · X, supervised.
Why this order matters: the "why now" narrative the trigger emits becomes the campaign's opener. Discovery that carries its own reason-to-reach makes activation convert — the two add-ons are worth more together than apart, but each still sells alone.
