## Enforcement reality since June 2024

### ESMA/Commission compliance guidance
ESMA statement of **17 January 2025** (ESMA75-223375936-6099): CASPs to restrict
services supporting **acquisition** of non-compliant ARTs/EMTs by end-January 2025,
"sell-only" permitted until **31 March 2025**. Custody and transfers of non-compliant
stablecoins are not themselves restricted.

### USDT delistings (Tether declined to seek EMT authorization)
- **Coinbase** — announced EEA delisting Dec 2024 (final cleanup by ~31 Mar 2025; dates vary by source — flagged)
- **Crypto.com** — USDT off for EU customers by 31 Jan 2025
- **Kraken** — sell-only from 24 Mar 2025, full delisting 31 Mar 2025
- **Binance** — delisted spot pairs for nine non-compliant stablecoins (USDT, FDUSD, TUSD, USDP, DAI, AEUR, UST, USTC, PAXG) for EEA by 31 Mar 2025, auto-conversion to USDC
- Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino's stated objection: the ≥60% EU-bank-deposit rule for
  significant tokens creates bank-run/systemic risk. USDT (~$186B, world's largest
  stablecoin) has no compliant route onto EU-regulated venues.

### Authorized issuers (EMT white papers notified)
- **Circle** — first global issuer compliant: **EMI licence, ACPR France, 1 July 2024** (USDC + EURC, passported EU-wide)
- **Société Générale-FORGE** — EURCV (credit-institution licence)
- **Quantoz Payments** (NL) — EURQ, USDQ; **Banking Circle** (LU) — EURI; **Membrane Finance** (FI) — EUROe; **StablR** (MT) — EURR; Paxos-linked USDG also cited (entity details unverified)
- Authoritative list: ESMA public MiCA registers

### EBA technical standards
Main MiCAR package published **13 June 2024**: own-funds RTS (2%→3% for significant
issuers), reserve liquidity RTS (maturity ladders: ~20%/40% one-day, 30%/60%
five-day sellability), recovery/redemption-plan guidelines, means-of-exchange
classification RTS (EBA/RTS/2024/13). **Aug 2025 dispute:** Commission moved to
endorse the liquidity RTS *with softening amendments*; EBA publicly objected they
were "inconsistent with MiCA" and would introduce material liquidity risk.

## The multi-issuance controversy (2025-2026) — the defining fight

**Structure:** the same fungible token issued by an EU-authorized entity and a
non-EU affiliate (Circle's global USDC model). Not foreseen by MiCA.

- **ECB position** (non-paper to Council, April 2025 — WK 4742/2025): in a run,
  holders worldwide redeem where protection is strongest (the EU, at par, fee-free),
  but EU-held reserves may be insufficient; multi-issuance also lets Article 23-style
  caps on foreign-currency tokens be circumvented. **Lagarde** forcefully opposed
  multi-issuance absent equivalent third-country safeguards.
- **Commission position:** MiCA's redemption obligation applies only to EU-minted
  tokens; multi-issuance permissible, possible codification via equivalence/
  reciprocity/ring-fencing amendments. (A mid-2025 Commission interpretive notice is
  referenced in press but no primary document surfaced — flagged.)
- **ESRB Recommendation (25 Sept 2025**, OJ C/2025/6342): Commission should clarify
  that third-country multi-issuer schemes are **not permissible** under MiCAR, or
  subject them to strict safeguards; amend Arts. 43/56 to make such schemes a
  significance criterion.
- **MiCA review:** Commission targeted consultation launched **20 May 2026**
  (86 questions, deadline 31 Aug 2026), feeding the Art. 140/142 review reports
  (full report, possibly with legislative proposal, due 30 June 2027).

## ECB / official-sector interpretations

- **Piero Cipollone** (ECB Executive Board, April 2025, European Parliament +
  "Digital euro: the future of money"): US promotion of dollar stablecoins could pull
  "euro deposits… to the United States," strengthen the dollar's role in cross-border
  payments, and compromise Europe's monetary sovereignty; the digital euro is the
  counterweight; 65% of euro-area card payments already run on non-European schemes.
- ECB blog "From hype to hazard: what stablecoins mean for Europe" (Schaaf, 28 Jul
  2025); speech "Stablecoins and monetary sovereignty" (18 Oct 2025); FSR box (Nov
  2025) "Stablecoins on the rise: still small in the euro area, but spillover risks
  loom"; working paper 3199 finding measurable deposit substitution.
- **Christine Lagarde** (Fortune, July 2025): stablecoins risk the "privatization of
  money" — "I regard money as a public good." (CoinDesk, 8 May 2026): Tether/Circle
  dominance risks "**digital dollarisation**" of Europe.
- Internal EU split: Banque de France's **Denis Beau** (May 2026) urged a
  public-private tokenized euro (backing the bank consortium Qivalis) rather than
  pure defense.
- **Market share:** euro-denominated stablecoins ≈ **0.15-0.2%** of the global market
  (~€450-500M circulation vs ~99% USD-pegged); EURC ~50-60% of the euro segment.

## Think-tank / academic interpretations

- **Bruegel** — "A new strategy to contain stablecoin risks in the EU" (May 2026):
  EU direction favors tokenized deposits/bank-issued digital money; documents the
  Commission-vs-ECB split; Europe-based stablecoin transactions were 38% of global
  volume in Q4 2025.
- **CEPS/ECRI** (Judith Arnal, Sept 2025) — "Multi-issuance stablecoins and MiCA's
  first real credibility test."
- **CEPR VoxEU** — "Multi-issuer stablecoins: a threat to financial stability" (run dynamics).
- **Oxford Business Law Blog** (Mar 2026) — MiCA's interest ban vs the US approach as
  a competitive fault line.
- **Blockchain4Europe** (Apr 2026) — "Reforming MiCA for Euro Stablecoins": industry
  reform agenda (interest ban and reserve rules as competitiveness drags).
- Law-firm guides: Norton Rose Fulbright (practical guide), White & Case, A&O
  Shearman (prudential series), Latham & Watkins MiCA tracker, Freshfields (2026 review).
